EdgePacer on your own data page

A Connect IQ data field cannot switch pages, so EdgePacer lives in one spot of the page you already ride with (here the top half of an Edge 540 page, your other fields below). It shows your 3s power until a segment of 2 % or steeper starts, paces you through it, shows the result for 5 s, then goes back. Numbers: your “last climb to longpan rd”, PR 1:42.
